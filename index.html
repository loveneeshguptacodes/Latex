Compile the files

  1. Download the source and its supporting files.
  2. Upload them into one Overleaf project or keep them in one local folder.
  3. Choose the correct main file and compiler.
  4. Click Recompile or run the commands in README.txt.

Important: Practicals 8 and 9 need the legacy devnag and itrans preprocessors plus their font resources. They are not ordinary single-file LaTeX builds.
